# Analysis

## Layer 8, Head 10

This head often connects a direct object back to the verb that governs it.
The strong cells from `pipe` to `lit` and from `book` to `read` suggest that
the head has learned part of the verb-object relationship.

Example Sentences:
- Holmes lit the pipe.
- She read the book.

## Layer 9, Head 6

This head often connects the object of a preposition to that preposition. In
these examples, `chair` attends strongly to `on`, while `door` attends strongly
to `at`. This can help the model represent prepositional phrases.

Example Sentences:
- The cat slept on the chair.
- We waited at the door.
